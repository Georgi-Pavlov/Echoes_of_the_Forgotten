# Ideas backlog (liked, to be explored later)

Ideas the project owner likes but has not designed or built yet. When one is explored, give it its own design section or doc, and move it out of here once it ships.

## Battle
- **Terrain and battlefield effects.** Tiles like mist, fire, ice, high ground and walls make movement choices matter more. Zones bring their own hazards: Everfrost gets slippery ice, Blackbloom gets poison spores, Drowned Empire gets flooded tiles. Each zone already has a `BattleAtmosphere`, so the visuals are partly there.
- **Elite and affixed creeps.** Random modifiers such as "Armored", "Vampiric" or "Splits on death" make stages vary between runs. Can be tied to world events (an event spawns elites).
- **Undo move.** One free undo per battle: forgiving without being exploitable.

## Progression and itemization
- **Skill upgrades or branches.** At certain levels the player picks between two versions of a skill (e.g. "Barbed Lunge: +range" or "Barbed Lunge: bleed"), so two runs of the same hero feel different. Must respect the whole-number damage rule.
- **Item sets and rarities (Diablo 2 style).** Set bonuses, cursed items with a trade-off, and rare stock that rotates over time. A small number of shop slots refresh as the world ticks, tying the shop to the simulation.
- **Consumable crafting or enchanting.** A late-game gold sink, such as socketing a gem or enchanting a weapon.

## Living world
- **Rival rivalries and alliances.** Rivals form alliances or grudges in the background; Restore and Rule rivals can cooperate. Shown as news on the map.
- **World events.** Timed events (corruption surge, mist storm, traveling merchant) change a zone's difficulty or shop for a while and add variety to "While you were away". May be tied to the spawn of elite creeps.
- **Intercepting invasions.** The player sees a rival heading for a zone and can race to defend it, giving a reason to act beyond clearing stages. Can tie in with rival alliances (e.g. allied rivals invade together).

## Replayability
- **New Game+ and difficulty modifiers.** After an ending, restart with harder rivals who start stronger or already hold echoes. Optional modifiers (no potions, permadeath items) score higher.
